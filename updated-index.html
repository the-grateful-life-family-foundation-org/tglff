Our Mission

To help humans be better people by changing the world, one mind at a time. We achieve this by providing tools and methodologies to raise awareness of others' needs and of our part in 'the great circle of life'.

Our Philosophy

  • We are 'one' and our actions affect others
  • Every person has esteemed value, assuming good intentions
  • Seeking reconciliation through forgiveness
  • Co-creating love and inclusivity
  • Collective consciousness is our source and supply

Our Location

Lake Waldensia is a 372-acre wooded property in Ozone, TN, serving as a nomadic haven and a place for tranquility, adventure, and connection. Located on Millstone Mountain Road, Rockwood, TN 37854, visitors can explore local history and culture while connecting with nature.

Save Lake Waldensia: Secure 372 Acres Forever

Now we are in the home stretch — our urgent priority: raise $250,000 by December 31, 2026 to pay off the land and place it in trust, protected forever

Where It All Began

I came out to look at a piece of land we'd never own… or that's what I thought. That's all it was supposed to be — 372 acres in Rockwood, Tennessee, and my friend Jacob Farr was walking me around it like any other listing. But I hadn't been on that property 15 minutes when I heard the ancestors calling me. Jacob had taken me down to Mammy's Creek at the bottom of the dam, to witness history pouring over the old stone dam the way it has for longer than any of us have been here. This was the history of this land.

I didn't have a plan in that moment. No foundation, no vision, no name for any of it. I just knew I wasn't looking at real estate anymore. The place had claimed me. Everything since — the Grateful Life Family Foundation, the community rising on that land, the challenges we face to protect it forever — traces back to those 15 minutes. Now we are in the home stretch, raising $250,000 by December 31, 2026, to pay off the land and place it into a land trust, managed by the Foundation, so it stays in community hands forever — yours, mine, and all who may come here looking for a trace of their history. Some of that history is in our gallery, and much of it has been shared with us by others who've come and gone but are still a part of that history. By becoming a founding partner now, you become a part of that history forever and help assure that future continues.

People ask why I'm doing this. The honest answer is simple: the land called, and I answered. Maybe you can hear it calling too. Come out and listen for yourself — stand beneath those falls. Listen to the past telling the future…

— Will Tritt, of the Grateful Life Family Foundation and the Lake Waldensia Land Trust

I personally invite you to help save Lake Waldensia and become a part of that future as a member of the Lake Waldensia Land Trust.

What Your Gift Does

  • About $200,000 pays off the 372 acres, which then go into a land trust managed by the Foundation
  • About $50,000 starts Phase 2: the first tiny homes and a bathhouse/office
  • Every gift is a tax-deductible donation to a 501(c)(3) — EIN 85-3347709

Two Ways to Become a Founding Partner

  • 1% Partner: a $20,000 contribution — you become an anonymous guardian of the Lake Waldensia Land Trust and qualify to host one of its themed camping areas. Thirteen partners at this level cover the entire goal. Partners don't buy the land — you fund the payoff and the project, and the land is protected, forever
  • Land Steward equity: earn the same 1% Partner standing through service to the land trust and campground — 2,222 hours, valued at $9/hour. A minimum of about 444 hours a year over five years, with the freedom to concentrate your hours in the spring and fall when camping on the land is least challenging. Finish in as little as two years if you prefer — build, grow, host, and teach — while you gain permanence and create symbiosis: your hands shape this place, as it shapes you

Trail of Trees

"Rooted in remembrance, the Trail of Trees honors those who walked the Trail of Tears by planting a living legacy across the lands they once crossed."

Beginning in Ozone, Tennessee, at The Grateful Life Family Foundation, saplings serve as symbols of healing, unity, and life.

"Every tree tells a story. Every step revives a memory. Every donation plants a future."

Our Purpose

  • Turning pain into purpose
  • Cultural remembrance
  • Climate regeneration

Native Trees We Plant

  • Red Cedar - for ecological value and historical significance
  • White Oak - supporting carbon offset
  • Dogwood - for habitat restoration
  • Persimmon - promoting public healing
